GET CONNECTED

Enter the world.

Dudgeon WoW uses the Wrath of the Lich King 3.3.5a client. Once you have a compatible client, connecting only requires the realm configuration below.

01

Prepare the client

Use a compatible World of Warcraft 3.3.5a client (build 12340).

02

Set the realmlist

Place the realm hostname in your client configuration:

set realmlist wowclient.dudgeon.io
03

Launch & log in

Start the client with Wow.exe and sign in with your realm account.

i
Account creation

Public self-service registration is not enabled on this site yet. When account tools are added, they can live here without exposing the AzerothCore database directly.

ABOUT

A home for the realm.

This site is the public front door for Dudgeon WoW: status, server information, connection instructions, configuration downloads, and eventually news, changelogs, account tools, and additional realm services.

The game service itself remains separate at wowclient.dudgeon.io, keeping the public website independent from the AzerothCore host.
